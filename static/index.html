The call key is created in your browser and never sent to Campfire or LiveKit. Share the call link out of band with the people you trust; anyone holding it can decrypt this call.

NOTIFICATIONS

What you get told about

A desktop notification names the channel and who wrote, never what was said, because it can appear on a lock screen. Muted channels still collect unread markers — quietly.

MODERATION

COMMUNITY SETTINGS

Community

Retention deletes old history on a schedule. Nothing is removed until you choose a window, and shortening one takes effect immediately.

Images can be kept for less time than words: they are most of the disk a small instance uses. Removing a shared image removes the message carrying it, because that message is the image.

CHANNEL RULES

Every member of the community can still read this channel. These rules govern contributing to it.

Slow mode limits how often one member may post. Moderators and above are not held to it, because they are who answers a flood.

ACCOUNT SECURITY

Sign-in and sessions

Passkeys

Use your device, password manager, or security key for phishing-resistant sign-in. Your current password remains the recovery method.

Active sessions

Campfire stores only when each session began and expires—not your device, IP address, or location.

Your data

A single JSON file holding your account, communities, messages, image details, and preferences. It is plain text and depends on nothing Campfire hosts.
